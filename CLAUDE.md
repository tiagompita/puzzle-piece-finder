# Puzzle Piece Finder

A computer-vision tool for a physical jigsaw (Clementoni 3000 "The Alps", ref. 33545; grid 64 × 47 = 3008 pieces, 218 border pieces, nominal cell ≈ 18.5 × 17.9 mm). Canonical input is a **flatbed scan at 300 dpi** of loose pieces on saturated-colour paper, spaced ≥ 5 mm (absolute scale: 1 px = 0.0847 mm). CLI (`src/main.py`, naive only) and a Tkinter GUI (`src/gui.py`).

**Symmetry (verified 2026-10-30):** the grid is 64 × 47 (confirmed by counting) and the die is symmetric under a 180° rotation: piece (l, c) has the same cut as piece (48 − l, 65 − c), rotated 180°, giving 1504 twin pairs and no self-twin. Measured on 20 border pairs: twin RMS 0.29 mm (same pose) / 0.39 mm (opposite pose) against 0.27 mm for a re-scan of the same piece and ≥ 0.60 mm for the nearest non-twin (`docs/diagnostico/gemeos/RESULTADOS.md`). Twins are physically interchangeable (one fits where the other belongs), so **only the image tells them apart: the image tie-break is mandatory**. Assembled sections: the front scanned at 300 dpi is enough; the back is optional (only 65–70 % of the cut length shows as a dark line there, the rest is relief; 600 dpi adds nothing).

**Sources of truth** (read these before planning anything): [`docs/estado-e-plano.md`](docs/estado-e-plano.md) (state with evidence, root cause, milestones, metrics, photos needed, backlog) and [`ARCHITECTURE.md`](ARCHITECTURE.md) (real module map, contracts, entry points, data). `docs/revisao.md` holds the review thread. Diagnostic evidence and reproducible scripts: `docs/diagnostico/` and `tools/diagnostico/`. The README is outdated and only carries a pointer.

## Current Objective (decided 2026-10-29, revised 2026-10-30)

**Objective (2026-11-01):** a working, intuitive program that helps Tiago finish this puzzle from random scanned lots ("I scan pieces at random and the program helps me assemble"), with no zone priority. Suggestions in the same flow: (a) place on the board where it fits now (placed twin, hole mould, frontier); (b) loose pieces that fit each other even without a board place; (c) loose groups located in the box image. Measured evidence decides the order. **Final goal (bonus):** phone photos of the art and of lots, and the program says where each piece goes (printed ArUco sheet as capture base, a new modality in the profile, [NT]); not now.

Legacy already scanned (2026-11-01): `images/pieces/legado/` montado_p01-p15 (about 633 placed pieces, exploded view) + blocos_p01-p03 (75 loose grouped); the 8-sheet grass protocol is superseded.

**Primary: find the twin.** For a piece placed at (l, c), find its twin among the loose scanned pieces (the cell (48 − l, 65 − c)): shape (side chords, 180°-invariant free distance) plus topological class, with the **image tie-break mandatory** (the twin's cell is known, so it is a 1-against-1 test against the art, not a search). Pairs inside the batch and an incremental index (placed pieces leave the pool) keep the effective pool far below 3008. The shape alone is viable up to about 60 candidates and not enough at 3008 (margin extrapolation in §6 of the plan). Product flow (§0 of the plan): incremental scans → persistent piece index + board map → suggestions ordered twin → hole mould → box (textured only) → loose-loose fit → confirmation in the GUI updates the map and spawns new queries.

**Secondary: assisted assembly by piece fit and hole mould.** Given an already-assembled section (photo/scan) and a batch of loose pieces (scan), rank which loose pieces attach to each free side of the section's frontier and where, and identify the piece that belongs in a hole (the right one or its twin). The side is the unit; the die has 5905 internal cuts, about 2953 distinct under the symmetry (interactive query, O(N) per query; no global 3000-piece solver). The assembled section is the local high-resolution reference.

**Tertiary: locating a loose piece in the box image** (the old primary goal) is kept only as a weak prior for textured pieces, reported with honest confidence. Measured: 72 % top-1 on synthetic self-crops, 0/61 confirmable on real scanned pieces. Cause (corrected 2026-10-30): the scanner is faithful to the art (uniform L\* shift of about +5), and the 61 July pieces really are light sky/cloud pieces without texture (art limit) compared against a box photo with a margin; photometric normalization (M9) reduces to a shift of about −5 L\*. The 61 July pieces were pre-filtered by Tiago (the ones he could not place): the L\* 86 is selection bias and the 0/61 is a worst-case sample; performance on random pieces is untested.

- Cadeia atual: **helper (Sonnet) → implementer (Opus)**. Começa-se no nível mais barato que consiga fazer a tarefa; só se escala para o implementer quando o helper não chega.
- Subagentes não lançam subagentes: «escalar» é parar e dizer ao gestor o que falta e porquê.

- **Cabeçalho dos prompts do gestor (desde 2026-11-06):** cada prompt abre com `Agente: <helper | implementer>. Skills: <...>.` A delegação a subagentes é decisão do modelo, a não ser que o Tiago faça @-mention do agente (`@agent-implementer` / `@agent-helper`, escolhido na lista ao escrever `@`), que é o único modo de a garantir; quando o prompt traz «Agente: X» e a sessão não lançou o subagente X, pára e diz porquê em vez de fazer o trabalho no gestor. Skills do projeto: `cv-visual-verification` em qualquer mudança de CV, `piece-fit-matching` em encaixe/gémeo; `systematic-debugging` em bugs. Quando o gestor quer plano antes de implementar, a PRIMEIRA linha do prompt é `/plan`; sem `/plan` não há plano.
